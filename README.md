# Saudi Market Trends

App for **Saudi business owners** that detects **short-term**, **long-term**, and **seasonal** market trends from open / OSINT sources, social platforms (X, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat), and KSA-scoped Google Trends — and turns them into product-discovery recommendations.
